Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Discord bot to use this app?
Yes, you'll need a Discord bot with the appropriate permissions. Our setup guide walks you through creating one in about 2 minutes — no coding needed.
Can I restrict linking to paying customers only?
Yes. Enable the "paid users only" option in settings and only customers with at least one completed paid order will be able to link their Discord account.
What happens if a customer unlinks their Discord?
Roles assigned by the app are automatically removed when a customer unlinks. You can also configure the app to keep roles even after unlinking.
Is there a free trial on paid plans?
Yes — the Pro plan includes a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. You can cancel at any time before the trial ends.
Does it work with Shopify's customer accounts?
Yes. The Discord link button appears on the customer account profile page using Shopify's Customer Account UI Extensions.
